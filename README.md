# Awesome Life Science Tools

A curated directory of high-quality web tools, software, and calculators designed to streamline workflows in life sciences, molecular biology, and chemistry.
> **A Note on Commercial Tools:** While several of the linked tools are hosted by commercial businesses, these resources are freely shared with the public. Centralizing them here welcomes more researchers to utilize these powerful utilities to optimize their daily laboratory workflows.

## Chemical Structure & Reagent Search
*Tools for looking up properties, safety details, and commercial chemical inventory.*

* [Advanced Chemical Search](https://www.macklin.cn/en/advsearch) – A powerful cheminformatics and structural search engine. Beyond standard text queries, it features an interactive molecular sketching canvas, two-way SMILES/CAS conversions, and an **Image-to-Structure** feature. This allows researchers to paste or upload an image of a chemical structure (as seen in `image_245de1.png`) to automatically generate editable digital models, eliminating the need for manual bond-by-bond drawing.
* [PubChem](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) – Open chemistry database at the National Institutes of Health (NIH) for looking up chemical structures and biological activities.

## Laboratory Calculators
*Interactive digital tools to handle routine lab math accurately.*

* [Buffer Calculator](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/GB/en/support/calculators-and-apps/buffer-calculator) – Computes exact mass requirements for preparing specific biological buffer solutions.

## Experimental Protocols & Methodologies
*Step-by-step laboratory execution guides and product-specific assay guidelines.*

* [Scientific General Protocols](https://www.aladdinsci.com/eu_gb/faqs/protocols.html) – A comprehensive central repository of laboratory protocols, covering common molecular biology, cellular assay, and analytical chemical methods.
* [Scientific Protein Affinity Tag Guide](https://www.aladdinsci.com/eu_gb/faqs/tags/protein.html) – A detailed technical guide on protein purification tags (such as His-tag, GST-tag, and Flag-tag), detailing specific elution protocols and troubleshooting workflows.

## Interactive Reference Tables
*Quick-access scientific charts and elemental datasets.*

* [Scientific Interactive Periodic Table](https://www.aladdinsci.com/eu_gb/periodic/) – A digital, click-to-explore periodic table of elements, optimized for lab researchers to quickly extract atomic mass, electron configurations, and fundamental chemical properties.
